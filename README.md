Project Lab is a place to test ideas, learn by doing, play games, and revisit older projects. Try Evolution Simulator and the Place Naming Game in the [Project Showcase](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/#showcase-title), or browse the four sections under [Explore](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/#explore-title). Use the search bar to jump to a project or section by name.

## [Experiments](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/experiments/)

### [Evolution Simulator](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/experiments/evolution-simulator/)

Watch blobs hunt, gather food, and pass on traits like size and speed. Build a species from a preset, then use the Species button in World to bring in newcomers or split a living group. Give each species its own diet, then use the meal table or replay to see what it ate.

## [Learning](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/learning/)

### [Place Naming Game](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/learning/places-game/)

Race the clock or practice at your own pace by naming places with or without their countries. Pick your filters and spelling rules, then use Preferences to save setups, set what happens when you switch away, or see separate timed and practice scores. For a hint, try up to eight random examples or search by country, letters, name length, and words to include or skip.

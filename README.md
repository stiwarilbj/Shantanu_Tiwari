Try Evolution Simulator and the Place Naming Game in the [Project Showcase](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/#showcase-title), or browse the four sections under [Explore](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/#explore-title). Use the search bar to jump to a project or section by name.

## [Experiments](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/experiments/)

### [Evolution Simulator](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/experiments/evolution-simulator/)

Watch blobs hunt, gather food, and pass on traits like size and speed. Build a species from a preset, then use the Species button in World to bring in newcomers or split a living group. Give each species its own diet, then use the meal table or replay to see what it ate.

## [Learning](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/learning/)

### [Place Naming Game](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/learning/places-game/)

Race the clock by naming places with or without their countries, and earn a point for each new answer even with a small typo. Set a time limit and filters, save favorite setups and see recent scores in Preferences, or resume a round after a break. Get up to eight random examples or search by country, letters, name length, and words to include or skip.

# Evolution Simulator visual and interaction design

[Open the simulator](https://stiwarilbj.github.io/Shantanu_Tiwari/experiments/evolution-simulator/) · [Walkthrough and implementation plan](PLAN.md)

The field is the brightest object on the page. Leave dark space around it so a tiny pellet or a moving blob still catches the eye. In Trends, give that job to the data. Settings changes the experiment and Compare shows what those changes did. These four views use the same blue surfaces, labels, and focus colors.

The video establishes the visual order and pacing. At 1:05 the board is a bright, angled tabletop with small olive dots. At 5:10 the charts are flat, with colored lines and soft bands around the averages. At 7:59 a small settings window sits above the dimmed results. At 8:43 two boards are visible at once and the brighter board draws the eye. Project Lab uses its own cooler colors, drawings, and board layout for those interactions. ## Color, type, and spacing

| Element | Color | What the visitor sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stage and reading surfaces | #132437 | A solid navy field surrounds the simulation and sits behind the charts and lessons. There is room for the board to rotate without crossing decorative panels |
| Generations dock | #0D1A28 | A deeper band anchors the bottom edge. A hairline separates it from the world |
| Raised panels, menus, and chart tooltips | #182C40 | Slate blue makes a surface read as being above the stage. The dark tool wells and text fields use #101E2D. Inset labels and hovered tool groups use #20364B |
| Text | #F4F8FD main, #E4ECF6 headings, #B7C8D9 labels, #9CB1C7 secondary | Near-white numbers and titles, cooler captions, and subdued hints. Chart axes use the lighter label color even in compact mode |
| Rules and borders | #36536D standard, #294054 subtle | One-pixel blue lines divide control strips, fields, plot axes, and readout rows |
| Actions and selection | #285B89 action, #336D9C action hover, #224865 selected | Settings and Evolve use the primary fill. Pressed controls and the current generation get a deeper blue fill. Secondary buttons use dark input blue and a thin outline |
| Keyboard and progress | #B0DDFF focus, #76C5EB rail | A 2 px focus ring uses pale ice blue. Playback fill, progress accents, and pressed borders use cyan |
| Overlays and error states | #081422CC scrim, #FFA89D validation text | A blue-black scrim keeps the previous view recognizable around a modal. Error text accompanies the warm accent and identifies the field or action |

The shared Project Lab navbar is the lightest interface band. Its 112° gradient goes from #BDE4FF through #CFE2FF at the 48% mark to #E1D4FF, with a faint pearl highlight overhead and #1B242D lettering. The simulator touches it directly and runs to both side edges and the bottom of the viewport. Simulator tabs, run settings, and world tools sit inside that space. Their desktop controls are about 36–40 px tall and their icon buttons stay compact. The Project Lab edition uses Inter for the simulator tabs and run controls, Source Sans for labels and reading, and Latin Modern Mono for headings, exact values, seeds, and times. Chart labels are 11–12 px, with 17 px metric headings. Tool wells use a 10 px radius, the Settings sheet uses 12 px, and small fields use 5 px. Borders and fill changes do most of the work. The strongest shadow sits beneath a dialog. Near-white text has 6.68:1 contrast on a primary button and 5.17:1 on hover. Chart tick text has 9.19:1 contrast against the stage.

World uses a camera-sized stage with more room above the field than below it. Reading views use 24 px side gutters up to a maximum width of 1740 px. Below 700 px the gutters are 12 px and the main charts form one column. At intermediate widths, three columns become two at 1000 px. At widths of 560 px and below the Settings sheet fills the screen, and side-by-side Compare boards stack. Short viewports scroll inside the simulator when the dock needs more room. Reduced-motion preferences shorten CSS transitions and space out the staged camera changes.

## World and all its controls

| Component | Appearance, behavior, and feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Simulator tabs | World, Trends, and About sit in a dark outlined strip at the upper left. The selected view has a blue fill and brighter lettering. Compare appears here while a comparison is open |
| Settings and run status | A filled blue Settings button sits beside a dark outlined day and status chip. The chip shows the current day, run length, and state when available. Its popup leads with the run name, seed, and saved day, followed by Run Library, Compare runs, branch, live return when available, and Show tour |
| Rendering surface | The playable area is a matte #F3F7FB square above a shallow slab. The refuge is #C9D5E1, the top rim #A7B8CA, the lit side #849AB1, the shaded side #697F96, and the underside #5D7085. These are material colors shared by the WebGL and Canvas renderers. An optional dashed outline uses #577488. The gray band follows the recorded refuge rules. Older runs keep the geometry they were saved with |
| Blobs, pellets, and contact shadows | Species carry their assigned body color. Small #83A51E food pellets sit on the field and shadows keep them legible against the off-white. Canvas shades the pellets from #A9BA4D through the olive base to #647D22. Blobs use tiny dark eyes and a soft shadow. The selected ring uses #285B89. A purple #6A53A8 hover ring marks the creature under the pointer |
| Camera | A wide oblique board is the starting view. Drag rotates the free camera, wheel or pinch zooms, and selecting a blob brings the camera in toward it. The upper-right camera toggles Follow creature. View options offers Fit board, north-up Overhead, Full screen, Creatures, graphics quality, dimming other species, and a HUD. Fit resets the board framing in one action. Canvas fallback shows a small Canvas view note |
| Layers | Open the stacked-squares button for Sense range, Color by speed, Energy, and Food credits. A short cyan stroke and pressed state mark active layers. Mouse hover or focus shows an explanation. On phones and short stages, menu labels sit beside the icons and the panel scrolls where needed. More options exposes Selected creature only and Show refuges |
| Layer marks | Sight is a translucent #CEEAFE disc sized to the creature’s range. Speed coloring uses a labeled brown-to-red ramp with fixed endpoints across the run. Energy ranges from gray to gold. Food credit pips use olive #A1C33F for plants and warm #D38C80 for prey. Sense focus darkens the area beyond the followed creature’s reach |
| Creature readout | The flower puts age, speed, size, and sense around the selected blob’s exact values. A colored species dot, name, and ID sit under it. Click that caption or use Creatures to see details in a bordered side panel. Energy, food credits, distance from refuge, and state are live readings. The event list can be filtered and recorded events can be opened at their replay time |
| HUD and notices | The optional HUD puts Alive, Home, Plants left, Hunts, and Deaths in a small upper-left panel. Loading, empty, or failure messages use a compact notice with retry when available. The camera and details controls keep their accessible names even when only icons fit |
| Playback | Previous day, the round blue Play/Pause control, Next day, and Play through generations form a single group. Current play or pause state is also in the button’s name. Quick Sim, playback actions, and rate controls sit at the lower right. Actions cover stepping one tick, reaching the end of a day, and returning to the recorded frontier or live play when available. The rate menu shows a vertical scale and a button that resets to 1× |
| Time rail | A 2 px blue rule spans the stage. Cyan grows with elapsed time. Recorded days get a 7 px thumb and a larger seek target. Live runs report elapsed progress. Hover or focus reads seconds. Space toggles playback when focus is on the field |
| Generations | The dark ribbon has 66 px tiles on desktop and 57 px tiles below 700 px. The active tile gets a blue tab with a pointed top. A tile has its day number and a trait flower, pending marker, or extinction glyph. Selection opens the day; an inspection card and Exact values expose the readings. The legend explains colors and can collapse. Generation options switch between flowers and Mini line. Nearby buttons reroll, extend, or collapse the ribbon |
| Current Generation Stats | A thin blue rule separates the last row from the ribbon. A species picker changes the cohort. Population, Age, Age at Death, Speed, Size, and Sense Range all have a color tick and a tabular value. An unavailable reading is shown as a dash so it cannot be mistaken for zero |

## Trends and the chart language

The first row of charts is Population, Age, Speed. The second is Size and Sense Range. Population is cream #F2EBD1. Age is gold #F1CE66. Speed is coral #F1847D. Size is sea green #52CFAB. Sense Range is ice blue #70CFFF. Age at Death uses the separate neutral #DDDAD0. The dock, flowers, chart headings, and chart lines use the same metric families. Accent headings can be a paler shade of their metric, especially on coral and green.

| Component | Appearance, behavior, and feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Toolbar and run context | Species, range, and Jump to chart go first. Download and run actions sit alongside and wrap in narrow layouts. The run name, seed, and recorded boundary are printed below the first divider. The phase and spread labels stay near the plots |
| Plot surface | Compact plots sit directly on navy with blue axes and no grid. Larger plots can use faint horizontal guides where they help read a value. Each colored heading has a small matching Hide control and an expand button. Hidden plots can be restored by name |
| Line, band, and extrema | The bright mean defaults to a 2.2 px line. The shaded band represents one sample standard deviation above and below the mean when enabled. Thin, low-contrast lines show the outer range. Population starts with a single count line. Keys use a short color sample and text. Clicking a key toggles the series and strikes the disabled label |
| Day inspection | Hover shows a thin guide and a slate tooltip. The tooltip starts with Day and scope, then lists values and applicable spread. A pinned day gets a brighter pale-blue guide. A mouse click opens that day’s replay. Touch and Shift-click pin the day. With focus on the chart, Left and Right move by day, Home and End reach the ends, Space pins, and Enter opens the replay. Shift-drag brushes a date range |
| Expanded views and exports | A focused chart gets space for its axes, keys, and exact controls. The PNG export paints the navy stage behind the plot so the pale axes remain readable on a light page. CSV records the selected data range and scope |
| Related readings | Chart options expose the phase, scales, and alternate summaries. The sight comparison and age walkthrough use visible day and cohort labels. Trait Space adds the trait plot, histogram, and exact age breakdown for the chosen cohort. Expose the source, phase, days, and sample count with a comparison whenever they are available |

## Settings and the run library

Settings opens a centered slate sheet over the current view. It is up to 700 px wide and capped at 94% of viewport height or 820 px. The desktop inset is 18 px. A 24 px title and one line of instruction sit above the form. The close icon lives in a dark square at the upper right. A 3 px blur softens the view under the blue scrim. On phones, the form itself scrolls and the blue Evolve action sits in a pinned footer.

| Component | Appearance, behavior, and feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Blob Creatures | Species tabs lead with a colored dot and count. Select a tab to edit that species. The name, color swatch, species actions, and starting trait values belong to it. Disabled or unavailable inputs are muted |
| Number controls | A colored track and white handle make a value easy to nudge. Minus and plus adjust by a step. The adjacent numeric input is for an exact value. A focused group gets a pale border. An invalid one gets a warm border and an explanation beside the field |
| When they reproduce | Speed, size, and sight mutation each show their own metric tint, spread reading, and Mutate checkbox. The label indicates standard deviation or variance for the current rules. Unchecked traits mute their numeric controls |
| Each generation | Energy has a gold line and food an olive line. Food schedule switches between Constant, Ramp, and Step. Ramp and Step show Day and Plants rows with remove, add, and clear actions. Refill rewards opens separate plant and prey inputs and Equal rewards |
| Finally | Run name, days, seed, and a seconds field for timed days sit together. Time presets appear only when timed. Seed controls show roll and copy, with Copied feedback. The end rule and Watch live or Prepare and watch choices sit above Advanced Rules |
| Advanced Rules and commit | The advanced section reveals the model and predation choices that apply to the draft. Branched runs explain which days are kept. The footer holds Evolve and its current action. Close or Escape follows the save or discard path when applicable |
| Run Library and progress | A matching slate dialog contains searchable runs. Each row puts the run title, seed, and saved state first. Compare, results, replay, resume, and management actions appear where available. Rename and destructive actions have their own controls and confirmation where needed. Batch progress uses a cyan bar and explicit day counts, with cancel, retry, or Watch last day when those actions apply |

## Compare, About, and the walkthrough

| Component | Appearance, behavior, and feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Compare setup | Two labeled run selectors are the first controls. Cool blue #83B3CE identifies the left run and warm peach #F6AD73 the right. Selection and chart legends retain their Left and Right text. The summary gives founder, final, and change values when the experiment supports it. Missing or unfinished runs get plain instructions |
| Two boards and their controls | Each lane has its own day, camera, play button, and progress slider. Fit both boards resets the framing. The shared day control, scope, and playback bar apply across lanes. A dark badge on each board identifies the run, seed, day, and seconds. A thin rule separates the boards. Side by side changes to stacked on phones. Reveal mode clips both at a draggable divider |
| Emphasis and staged scenes | The foreground lane gets a 2 px colored edge and the other board renders at 80% brightness. The staged Day 6 and Day 77 sequence starts from a paused setup that visitors start explicitly. It takes over the simulator surface below the shared navbar and hides the simulator’s regular tabs and controls. Focus moves to Stop video sequence. Escape or Stop returns focus to Play video sequence and restores the surrounding controls |
| About chapter list | Start with the lab title, a short description, and a row of selectable chapters. The active chapter gets the same blue pressed state as the controls. The chapter card uses dark input blue, a centered title, and wide margins. The opening artwork, stride comparison, and energy equation use the same reading column |
| About references and experiments | Video tiles carry a clear play action and load the external player on request. Experiments use the same framed surfaces with their question, setup, and action. For the size experiment the selected run length, progress, and cancellation stay in the experiment’s section |
| Guided World | The example title, day, and return action sit above a contained board. A caption appears at the bottom of the image. Below the board are Play or Pause, reset, a seek control with exact seconds, and optional Sense focus. Multi-part scenes add labeled steps. The energy lesson has a narrow formula panel that moves below the field on phones |
| First-run tour | A dialog pairs the board with a chapter title, progress, short instructions, and a demo play or pause button. Close lets the visitor skip it and the run menu can reopen it |

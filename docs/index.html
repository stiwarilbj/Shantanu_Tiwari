    Project Showcase

    Place Naming Game → Name as many places as you can before time runs out
    More details

    Play worldwide or pick a country, then set the timer and choose starting and ending letters. Earn a point for each new place you name, even if you make a small typo. Get random examples or search for places by country, letters, name length, and words to include or skip.

    Evolution Simulator → Watch a population of blobs evolve over generations
    More details

    Set the food supply and decide how much traits like size, speed, and sight can change. Follow a blob through a day, skip ahead with Quick Sim, or replay a past generation. Use charts to track changes and compare runs side by side.

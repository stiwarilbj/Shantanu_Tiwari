    Navigation

    Top bar → Project Lab

    Project Lab
    Returns to the site homepage.
    Search projects and sections
    Type a name and open a suggestion. Use the arrow keys to move through results, Enter to open one, and Escape to close the list.
    Experiments, Learning, Games, Old Projects
    Select a section name to open it. Hover or use the keyboard to reach its project links and Filler page. On a narrow screen, open Menu first.

    Simulator header

    Blob icon and World
    Open the simulation field, playback controls, and generation timeline.
    About
    Opens the walkthrough, sample experiments, and this reference.
    Trends
    Opens the charts and Trait Space for the current run.
    Compare
    Appears when you are comparing two runs.
    Settings
    Opens Simulation Settings so you can set up an experiment.
    Run options
    Open the menu using the Day and Live or Replay label. It shows the run name, random seed, and how far results have been saved.
    Run Library

    Start a new run from a completed day while keeping the original run.

    Opens a short interactive introduction.

    Appears on keyboard focus and moves you to the main simulator view.
    Retry simulation, Retry replay, and Dismiss error
    These appear when something fails. Restart the simulation connection, reload a day, or clear the message.

    Reading the board

    World → Simulation field

    Playing surface
    This is where blobs move, look for plants, and hunt when the rules allow it. Change the surface, sides, and space around the board under Settings → Advanced → Styles → Board colors.
    Refuge border
    In runs with a refuge strip, the border on the top surface is home. Blobs that settle there stop moving for the day and are safe from hunting. To trace the boundary, open Layers → More layer options → Show refuges.
    Plant pellets
    Each dot is a plant a blob can eat if its diet allows it. The dot disappears when eaten. A new supply spawns at dawn, either scattered or in patches, depending on Field layout. Set their display color in Board appearance.
    Blob color and size
    Body color shows species by default. Set your own colors under Settings → Advanced → Styles → Species colors. Color by speed uses a separate palette. Board appearance scales every blob on screen. Open Creature Details for the exact Size trait.
    Faded or tipped-over blob
    After a blob dies, it briefly appears faded and can tip over in the angled view. Check Creature Details → Filter creature events → Deaths to see what happened.
    Camera and selection
    Drag to rotate the angled view. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Select a blob to follow it, or select empty field to clear the selection. You can also pick a blob by ID in View options → Creatures. Use Fit board if you lose your bearings.
    Arrow-key movement
    Click the World board or Tab to Board camera. Up and Down move forward and back, while Left and Right move sideways. Moving with the keys stops following a blob.
    Teal ring or red outline
    When visible, a teal ring marks the selected blob. A red outline marks the blob under the mouse.

    World → Signs and readouts on the board

    Creature thought bubble
    Appears over a blob you are following. Select it to read its current action. Dots mean searching, a leaf means heading for a plant, utensils mean hunting, wind means fleeing, an arrow means returning home, and zzz means resting.
    Creature flower and caption
    While following a blob, the flower shows its age in yellow, speed in red, size in teal, and sense range in blue. The caption shows its species and ID. Select it to open Creature Details.
    Sense circles and Sense focus
    Layers → Sense range draws pale blue circles showing how far blobs can detect food or danger. Sense focus, beside the followed blob’s caption, dims everything outside that blob’s range.
    Energy disk
    Layers → Energy adds a disk under each blob. Gold means more energy remains and gray means less. Creature Details gives the exact amount.
    Food-credit dots
    Layers → Food credits shows the meals each blob has eaten that day. Green dots count plants and rust-colored dots count prey. Each dot is one food credit.
    Run speed key
    Layers → Color by speed shows a brown-to-red scale with the slowest and fastest speed values. The same scale is used across the run.
    HUD counts
    Turn on View options → HUD. Alive counts all living blobs and Home counts those already settled. Plants left counts uneaten plants. Hunts and Deaths count events so far that day, up to the moment you are watching.
    Food season readout
    When Food seasons is enabled, the strip below the time bar shows the current phase and how many plants spawned at dawn. Use the HUD to see how many are left.

    World and playback

    World → Top-right controls

    Species
    Open the species editor. You can make changes during an unfinished live generation. Historical replay is read-only.
    Follow creature
    Toggle the camera following the selected blob. Choose a creature on the board if one is needed.
    Layers → Sense range
    Draw circles around blobs to show how far they can detect food or danger.
    Layers → Color by speed
    Color blobs by speed. The key runs from brown for slower blobs to red for faster blobs on a scale shared across the run.
    Layers → Energy
    Show the energy overlay. Gold means full and gray means spent.
    Layers → Food credits
    Show what blobs have eaten. Olive marks plants and rust marks prey. Each meal gives one food credit.
    Layers → More layer options
    Open Selected creature only to limit overlays to one blob, or Show refuges to mark the safe edges. Back to layers and Close layers leave those menus.
    View options → Fit board and Overhead
    Fit board resets the camera to show the field. Overhead looks straight down and stops following the selected blob.
    View options → Full screen
    Expand the field to fill the screen. Use Exit full screen or Escape to return.
    View options → Creatures
    Open or close Creature Details.
    View options → Graphics
    Pick Auto, Full, or Efficient to balance detail and drawing speed. Canvas view appears if the board uses its 2D fallback.
    View options → Dim other species and HUD
    Dim other species fades the other blobs when you pick a species in Current Generation Stats. HUD shows the field counts, including how many blobs have made it home.

    World → Playback, beneath the board

    Previous day and Next day
    Move one generation back or forward when that day is available.
    Play, Pause, and Replay day
    Start or pause live play or a replay. At the end of a replay, the same button restarts that day.
    Play through generations
    Keep going into the next day when the current one ends.
    Viewing speed
    Open the clock or multiplier button beside Quick Sim. Pick 1×, 2×, 4×, or 8×, or use the slider for a speed from ⅛× to 8×. This changes playback speed for live days and replays.
    Replay time
    The clock shows seconds elapsed and the day’s total length when known. Drag the bar or use the arrow keys to seek in a recorded day. During live play it shows progress through the day.
    Previous frame and Next frame
    While a replay is paused, move backward or forward by one recorded frame.
    Playback actions → Finish Day or Go to end
    Finish simulating the current live generation or jump to the final frame of a replay.
    Playback actions → Step one tick
    Advance by a single simulation step.
    Playback actions → Adjust living traits
    Pause at the live position and open a preview of trait changes for the creatures you choose.
    Playback actions → Go to frontier and Back to Live
    Go to frontier opens the last completed day. Back to Live moves to the current live position.
    Quick Sim or Progress
    Complete several generations in the background or check a batch that is already running.

    World → Creature Details

    Creature
    Choose a living blob by ID or select one directly on the field. Close creature details hides the panel.
    Copy stats
    Copies the creature’s ID, species, day, speed, size, and sense range as plain text. If clipboard access fails, copy the text shown in the panel.
    State, Foraging decision, and remaining energy
    See what the blob is doing, why it chose that action when a reason is available, and how much energy it has left. Returning blobs also show the distance to refuge.
    Age, Food credits, Speed, Size, and Sense range
    Read the creature’s current age, meals earned, and three inherited traits at the displayed moment.
    Travel rate, Body radius, and Energy cost
    See its actual movement speed, body radius in field units, and the cost of moving under the current rules.
    Born and Parent or Parents
    Show the birth day and the IDs of known parents.
    Filter creature events
    Choose all events or narrow to plants, hunts, refuge, deaths, births, trait adjustments, introductions, or species transfers. Select a replay event to jump to its time.

    World → Generations and Current Generation Stats

    Generation tiles
    Open a day by selecting its tile. Use the arrow keys to move between tiles and Home or End to reach the first or latest completed day. A day with only a forecast has no recorded replay yet.
    Generation flowers and center dots
    On a completed day’s tile, the colored petals show averages for the chosen species or all species. Longer petals mean higher values on the run’s scale. The pale center dot grows with population. Hover a petal or dot for its number, or open Day readings for all the values.
    Extinction tile and More
    The extinction tile opens the last scene. More or More Generations extends a run while the population can still continue.
    Legend and Exact values
    The legend explains the colors and lets you emphasize a metric. Its chevrons hide or show it. Exact values opens the generation table.
    Day readings
    Right-click a completed generation tile or focus it and press I to see its exact numbers. Use its bookmark button to save or remove the day. Close dismisses the readings.
    Generation options → Bookmark and Bookmarks
    Bookmark the completed day you are watching. Use Bookmarks to jump back to a marked day later, even after refreshing.
    Generation options → Walkthrough flower, All six metrics, and Mini line
    Change how the timeline summarizes generations.
    Generation options → Numeric table and Go to day
    Open the generation table or enter a day number and select Go.
    Reroll seed
    Opens the setup with a new random seed so you can try the same experiment with a different starting sequence.
    Collapse generations and Expand generations
    Hide or bring back the timeline to change how much space the board has.
    Generation statistics species
    Show all species or one species in the timeline and stats.
    Current Generation Stats
    Read population, age, age at death, speed, size, and sense range for the displayed day and scope.
    View generation table and Advanced stats
    The info icon opens the generation table. The chart icon opens survival, trait spread, and food statistics.

    Changes during a live day

    World → Playback actions → Adjust living traits

    Group and Activity
    Choose all species, one species, multiple species, or individual blobs. Limit the group to any activity, active in field, or settled in refuge.
    Choose species and Choose living blobs
    Check the species or creatures to change. Find by ID or species narrows the list. Selected only shows the blobs you have picked.
    Speed, Size, and Sense Range
    Enter a change from −100 to +100 points with up to two decimals. Leave a field at zero to keep that trait. Each trait has a minimum.
    Average traits before and after
    Preview how many living blobs will change and how their average traits will move.
    Apply adjustment and Cancel
    Apply records the change at the paused step. Cancel closes the draft. The change is visible in replays.

    World → Species

    Introduce species
    Choose a name, color, preset, and how many blobs to release. Open Customize traits for individual values, then use Add to field to release the new species at the paused step.
    Split living blobs and Source species
    Move selected creatures from an existing species into a new one. They keep their IDs, positions, age, energy, meals, and family history.
    Pick blobs, Number to move, and Find by ID
    Use Seeded sample to pick a repeatable number of blobs or Choose by ID to select specific creatures.
    Edit species and Species to edit
    Change an existing species’ name or color and optionally adjust its living traits.
    Hue
    Drag the slider or use the arrow keys to shift a species’ color. The swatch previews it. Apply changes records the new color at the paused step.
    Living traits → Change type
    Check Speed, Size, or Sense Range and either Add or subtract points or Set exact values. See the affected creatures in the preview.
    Food web diet
    Set the diet for a new or split species. A split copies the source diet. A choice for its own species follows the split group and other prey choices keep their original targets.
    Edit food web
    Change diets and test encounters in one draft at the displayed generation and step. Apply changes records a single edit. Discard leaves the web as it was.
    Creature ID preview
    Expand the preview to see the IDs about to be introduced or changed.
    Apply changes, Cancel, Done, Make another change, and Resume
    Apply records the edit. Cancel closes the draft. After applying, Done leaves the world paused, Make another change opens a fresh draft, and Resume continues the day.
    Close and Back to Live
    Exit a read-only view or return from a replay to the live day to edit.

    Basic settings

    Simulator header → Settings → Basic → Blob Creatures

    Species tabs
    Pick which species to edit. A tab shows its founder count or Off.
    New species and Duplicate
    Start with a suggested name, color, and the Balanced preset, or copy the selected species.
    Species actions → Move left, Move right, Remove, and Undo
    Reorder species in the setup, remove one from the draft, or restore the last removal.
    Name, Color, and Enabled
    Choose a species label and swatch. Turning Enabled off leaves it in the setup without placing its founders.
    Hue
    Shift the selected species’ color while keeping its lightness and saturation. Use the Color swatch to start from another shade.
    Founders
    Set the number of blobs of this species at the start of a run, from 10 to 1,000. A live introduction can start with as few as one blob.
    Energy
    Set each founder’s energy budget from 10 to 1,000. The card estimates travel speed and how long that budget lasts. Energy and food reset at dawn.
    Speed, Size, and Sense Range
    Set each starting trait from 1 to 100. Speed changes movement, size affects hunting and sometimes stride, and sense range changes how far blobs can detect nearby things.
    Balanced, Fast, Sturdy, and Wide sight
    Fill in starting traits using a preset when creating a species. Fast raises speed, Sturdy raises size, and Wide sight raises sense range.
    Customize traits
    Open the extra fields to change energy, speed, size, or sense range one at a time.
    Add to setup and Cancel
    Confirm a pending species or discard it. Then use Evolve! when the whole setup is ready.
    Reproduction and Inheritance & mutation
    Open the matching advanced settings directly from Basic.

    Simulator header → Settings → Basic → Each generation and Finally

    Food
    Set the base daily plant count from 10 to 1,000. Food schedules and seasons can change that count for later days.
    Run name
    Name the experiment as it will appear in Run Library.
    Days to run
    Set the initial limit from 10 to 1,000 generations. Extinction can end a run earlier.
    Random seed
    Choose a whole number from 1 to 4,294,967,295. The same seed and rules reproduce the same result. Branches keep the seed from the original run.
    Roll seed and Copy seed
    Pick a new random seed or copy the current number.
    Prepare and watch
    Compute the archive in the background so completed days can be replayed.
    Watch live
    Run each simulation step while you watch and make live edits between steps.

    Simulator header → Settings → Tabs and footer

    Basic, Advanced, Advanced Settings, and All categories
    Switch between the setup and the advanced list. All categories returns to the list from an advanced topic.
    Number fields
    Type a value or use the minus and plus buttons to change it by one step. Hold and drag in the center strip to change it gradually. Drag farther from the middle to change it faster.
    Setup summary
    The footer totals enabled species and founders, and shows the number of base plants per founder.
    Discard changes, Discard draft, and Keep editing
    Open a confirmation, restore the setup you opened, or return to the draft.
    Evolve! and Create branch
    Start a run with the draft rules or apply them after a branch’s saved day. Review any field errors before submitting.
    Close settings
    Closes the settings sheet and takes you back to the simulator.

    Advanced · Field & food

    Settings → Advanced → Field & food → Plant supply

    Food schedule
    Constant keeps the base supply steady. Ramp changes it gradually between points. Step changes it on the day you set. Day 1 uses the Food value from Basic.
    Day and Plants
    Set a schedule point from Day 2 through the run limit. Enter 10 to 1,000 plants. Each point needs a different day.
    Add point, Remove schedule point, and Clear
    Add a point for an unused day, delete one using its trash button, or remove all points.

    Settings → Advanced → Field & food → Food seasons

    Enable food seasons
    Apply a repeating rise and fall to the base plant supply. Daily supply stays between 10 and 1,000 plants.
    Peak supply (%)
    Set the richest day to 100–200% of the scheduled supply.
    Lean supply (%)
    Set the leanest day to 0–100% of the scheduled supply.
    Generations from peak to leanest
    Set the length of the decline to 1–100 generations.
    Generations from leanest to peak
    Set the length of the recovery to 1–100 generations. The decline and recovery together make one cycle.
    First peak day in cycle
    Start the first peak at any day number within the cycle. The season repeats on that schedule.
    Food cycle
    Read the forecast plant count against the base schedule for a full cycle, with the preview day marked.

    Settings → Advanced → Field & food → Food types

    Enable food types
    Turn on seeds, berries, and mushrooms. This starts off. Switching back to one food type keeps your mix and species diets for later.
    Food mix
    Use whole weights from 0 to 100. A weight of zero stops that food from growing. The counts show how the scheduled supply splits at the start of a new run or branch. All three foods give the usual plant meal and energy reward.
    Species diets
    Pick a species and check any foods it can eat. New species can eat all three unless you give them a different diet. The Species food web still decides whether a species can eat plants at all.

    Settings → Advanced → Field & food → Field layout

    Food placement
    Scattered spreads plants across the field. Patches divide the day’s plant supply across clusters.
    Number of patches
    Choose 1–8 food clusters.
    Patch radius
    Choose a radius of 10–60 field units for the clusters.
    Patch relocation
    Fixed for the run keeps cluster centers in place. Move every N generations redraws the centers on a schedule.
    Generations between moves
    Set the move interval to 1–100 generations.
    Refuge width
    Set the safe border to 10%, 15%, or 20% of the field width, measured inward from each edge. Wider borders leave less growing space.

    Settings → Advanced → Field & food → Dawn preview in Plant supply, Food seasons, or Field layout

    Preview day
    Enter a day to see the number of plants at dawn, where they grow, the refuge border, and whether hunting is active. At or before a branch point, the preview uses the saved rules.
    Season reading and map
    The reading explains the current seasonal phase and any plant-count limit. The map shows the field with plants and patches.

    Settings → Advanced → Field & food → Starting territories

    Use starting territories
    Turn on planned dawn locations for creatures. Leave it off to use the normal dawn placement.
    Apply territories
    Opening generation only places creatures with these rules at the start. Every dawn places the population again on each new day.
    Placement for and Use custom settings
    Edit the Field default or choose a species. Turn on a species override to copy and edit the current field values. Turn it off to use the field again.
    Starting edges
    Check one or more of Top, Right, Bottom, and Left. Creatures can start along the selected refuge edges.
    Band center (%)
    Move the starting band along its edge from 0–100%. Horizontal edges read left to right. Vertical edges read top to bottom.
    Band width (%)
    Set the band to 5–100% of the edge. At a corner, it slides inward so it can keep its width.
    Dawn preview and Retry preview
    See the opening plant and creature positions with counts by species and edge. If a saved starting point fails to load, request it again.

    Advanced · Creature behavior

    Settings → Advanced → Creature behavior → Movement and energy

    Stride
    Size-linked makes larger blobs take longer strides. Speed only leaves size out of the stride calculation.
    Energy model
    Video walkthrough uses (size³ × distance per step² + sight range) ÷ 10,000 per 0.1-second step. Product uses size³ × speed² × sense ÷ 20,000 per second. Earlier additive uses 0.001 × (size³ × effective speed² + sense) per second.
    Energy calculation
    See the movement cost for the species selected in Settings and the formula that produced it.
    Refill energy after meals
    Allow food to restore energy up to the blob’s starting amount. Turn it off to keep your reward amounts saved while meals still earn food credits.
    Plant reward and Prey reward
    Set the energy restored by each meal to 0–1,000. A reward of zero leaves energy unchanged.
    Equal rewards
    Copy the plant reward to the prey reward.

    Settings → Advanced → Creature behavior → Foraging decisions

    Foraging rules
    Standard uses the decisions below. Video experiment uses its own step-based turning and dawn movement rules.
    Use custom foraging decisions
    Apply the field and species choices in Standard mode. Turning it off restores the built-in values and keeps your custom values saved.
    Foraging decisions for and Use custom settings
    Choose the Field default or a species. Turn on custom settings to start with a copy of the field values. Turn it off to inherit them again.
    Plant memory (seconds)
    Keep a targeted plant in mind for 0–3 seconds after losing sight of it. A blob drops the target sooner if it sees that the plant is gone. Default 0.6 seconds.
    Time saved to switch meals (%)
    Set the minimum time savings for switching targets. At 0%, switch to any strictly quicker meal. At 100%, keep a viable target. An exact tie keeps the current meal. Default 20%.
    Return safety margin (steps)
    Keep 0–20 extra simulation steps in reserve when deciding whether another meal still leaves time to get home. The panel shows the equivalent in seconds. Default 2 steps.
    Maximum prey pursuit (seconds)
    Set the longest estimated intercept to pursue, from 0–3 seconds. Zero stops deliberate pursuit and still allows eligible contact. Default 1 second.
    Try a decision → Situation
    Preview Plant leaves sight, Quicker meal appears, One more meal or head home, or Prey pulls away. The result uses the selected species and current settings.

    Settings → Advanced → Creature behavior → Hunting

    Predation
    Allow blobs to hunt one another when the day, size, and diet rules permit it.
    Same-species hunting
    Allow eligible blobs to hunt their own species. The food web can still block that hunt.
    Minimum hunting size advantage
    Set how much larger a hunter must be than its prey, from 5–100%. The example shows the biggest prey a size-10 hunter could eat.
    First hunting day
    Set the first generation when hunting is allowed. Before then, blobs can only eat plants if their diet allows them.

    Settings → Advanced → Creature behavior → Species food web

    Enable species food web
    Give each species its own diet. Off uses the usual feeding rules and saves your custom diets for later.
    Eating species
    Choose whose diet to view or change. Diets follow species IDs through renames. Allowing one species to hunt another changes only the eater’s diet.
    Eats plants
    Allow the selected species to eat plants. A species with no sources of food can still be used for an experiment.
    Can hunt and Allowed prey
    Any eligible species includes future arrivals. Selected species limits prey to the checked list. None blocks hunts. Predation, hunting day, size, same-species rules, and refuge protection still apply.
    Test an encounter → Food source and Generation
    Choose a plant or a prey species and the day to test. The preview explains the first rule that decides if the encounter is allowed.
    Test an encounter → Hunter size and Prey size
    Change the two sizes to test a prey encounter. With a 25% advantage, a size-15 hunter meets the cutoff for size-12 prey. The result describes eligibility for contact, since a catch still depends on movement.

    Advanced · Life cycle

    Settings → Advanced → Life cycle → Reproduction

    Allow reproduction
    Let creatures have offspring after reaching refuge with at least two food credits. Turning it off keeps your reproduction settings saved.
    Parent mode
    Single parent lets each eligible blob breed on its own. Two parents pairs eligible blobs of the same species at dusk, and each child starts with the average of its parents’ traits.
    Minimum breeding age
    Set how many full generations a blob must finish before it can breed, from 0–30. Zero allows breeding on its first day.
    Wait after a birth
    Skip 0–30 full generations after having offspring. A wait of 1 after a birth on Day 4 allows the next birth on Day 6.
    Chance of a birth (%)
    Give each eligible parent or pair one roll, from 0–100%. Zero turns off births while preserving the other rules.
    Chance of twins (%)
    Set the chance of a second child after a successful birth roll, from 0–100%.

    Settings → Advanced → Life cycle → Inheritance & mutation

    Species tabs
    Choose whose offspring can mutate. Enable births to edit the mutation settings.
    Mutation profile
    All unlocked allows changes to all three traits. Speed only locks size and sense range. All locked turns mutation off. Custom describes a mix you set yourself.
    Mutation pattern
    Bell curve favors small changes. Bounded random gives values within its range equal odds.
    Allow speed, size, or sense range mutation
    Turn mutation on or off separately for each trait.
    Speed, Size, and Sense Range mutation chance (%)
    Set the chance from 0–100% that each unlocked trait changes in a child.
    σ Speed, σ Size, and σ Sense Range
    Set the spread for each trait. A higher number allows larger changes. Current rules use standard deviation from 0–10. Some older runs use variance (σ²) instead.
    Variance (σ²) and Example offspring
    Variance is the square of standard deviation. The example shows possible offspring speed for the species you selected.

    Settings → Advanced → Life cycle → Aging and lifespan

    Aging rules for and Use custom settings
    Edit the Field default or choose a species. Give the species its own copy of the field values or turn the override off to inherit them again.
    Enable aging
    Apply old-age risk and speed loss to creatures using these rules. Blobs gain one year at dusk, before the death check and breeding.
    Aging starts after age
    Choose an age from 0–1,000 before aging effects start.
    Extra old-age death risk per generation
    Add 0–100 percentage points of risk for each generation past the starting age, checked at dusk.
    Travel speed lost per generation (%)
    Reduce travel speed by 0–100 percentage points per generation after aging starts. The movement energy cost stays the same.
    Minimum travel speed (%)
    Limit how far travel speed can fall, from 10–100% of normal.
    Set a maximum lifespan and Maximum lifespan
    Turn on a hard age limit, then choose from 1–1,000 generations.
    Age preview → Preview age
    Enter 0–1,000 to see travel speed, the risk of dying from old age, and whether the lifespan limit has been reached.

    Advanced · Run & charts

    Settings → Advanced → Run & charts → Day length

    End each day
    When all blobs settle or die ends the day when no active creature remains. After a fixed time also sets a time limit.
    Seconds per day
    Set a fixed day from 1–60 simulated seconds in tenths of a second. The 1, 5, 10, 20, 30, and 60 s buttons fill in common choices.

    Settings → Advanced → Run & charts → Graph predictions

    Show predictions
    Show or hide dashed future values on supported charts and the generation timeline. The chart needs at least three completed days in a row. Predictions use the seed and scheduled rules. Past the run limit, they are labeled If extended.
    Cycles ahead
    Choose 1–25 generations to predict. Press Enter or leave the field to save a valid number. This display preference applies immediately.

    Advanced · Styles

    Settings → Advanced → Styles → Board appearance

    Blob size
    Scale how large blobs look on the board from 50–200%. The default is 115%. Their Size trait, spotting range, and movement stay the same.
    Food size
    Scale how large food pellets look on the board from 50–200%. The default is 75%. The same rules still decide when a blob can eat.
    Food color
    Choose the color of food pellets on any board, including replays. The default is green.
    Reset styles
    Restore both display sizes and the default food color. These controls update every board immediately and save your choices in this browser.

    Settings → Advanced → Styles → Board colors

    Playing surface and Refuge border
    Recolor the playable field and the home strip around it. The size and rules of the refuge stay the same.
    Board base and Backdrop
    Choose colors for the rim and sides of the board and for the space around it.
    Refuge outline
    Set the color of the dashed boundary shown when Layers → More layer options → Show refuges is on.
    Reset board colors
    Restore the tabletop palette. Food and species colors keep their settings.

    Settings → Advanced → Styles → Species colors

    Species color picker
    Choose a display color for each species in this run. It also appears in replays and is saved in this browser. The run’s setup and the Color by speed palette stay the same.
    Reset
    Restore the recorded color for that species. The other species keep their display colors.

    Charts, stats, and Trait Space

    Trends → Top controls

    Filter By Species
    Show all creatures or focus the charts and readings on one species. Field-wide food counts still describe the whole field.
    Range, From day, To day, and Reset range
    Show the full run, last 25, last 100, or a custom range. Enter the first and last days for a custom range. Reset range shows the full run again.
    Jump to chart
    Scroll to the chosen chart.
    Generation table and Advanced stats
    Open the spreadsheet-style history or deeper statistics for survival, traits, and meals.
    Download
    Opens export options with the current date range.
    Run again and Reroll seed
